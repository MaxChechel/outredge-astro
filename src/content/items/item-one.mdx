<Lede>
  Repeating content lives in a collection from day one — not in a typed module
  that will become one later, because that migration never happens for free.
</Lede>

The adapter in `src/lib/items.ts` turns an entry into a view model, and the card
that renders this item has never seen a `CollectionEntry`. That is what makes
swapping `glob()` for a CMS loader a one-file change rather than a refactor.
